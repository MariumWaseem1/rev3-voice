Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This policy explains what information Rev3 collects through this website, why we use it and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, business name, email address, phone number and the information you include in your message. We may also receive basic technical information needed to operate and protect the website, such as request logs, browser type and approximate device information.

How we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to you, arrange demonstrations or meetings, understand what you are looking for and take steps towards providing our services. We use limited technical information to keep the website reliable, secure and working correctly.

Our legal reasons

Depending on the circumstances, we process information because you have asked us to take steps before entering into a contract, because it is necessary for our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and operating our website, or because you have given consent.

Who receives information

We only share information with service providers that help us operate the website and deliver enquiries, where necessary. These providers process information on our behalf under appropriate arrangements. We do not sell personal information.

Google Maps

Our contact section includes an embedded Google map. Google may receive technical information or place cookies when you load or interact with the map. You can avoid the embedded map by not interacting with it and use the written address instead.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage the relationship, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. Technical records are kept only for the period needed for security and service operation.

Your rights

Depending on the law and circumstances, you may ask for access to your information, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to its use. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent.

Contact and complaints

For privacy questions or requests, email info@rev3.uk. You may also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The date above shows when it was last revised.
