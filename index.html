[ FAQ ]

Questions, answered.

Will callers know they are speaking with AI?

Yes. Rev3 clearly identifies itself as an AI assistant at the start of the call.

Can Rev3 use our existing phone number?

In many cases, yes. Calls can be routed to Rev3 during the hours, overflow periods or conditions you choose.

What happens when Rev3 cannot handle a request?

It follows your handoff rules, transfers the call where available or creates a clear callback request with the details collected.

Can it book directly into our calendar?

Yes, when connected and approved. Rev3 can check availability, create the booking and send a confirmation.

Can Rev3 answer calls outside business hours?

Yes. You choose when Rev3 answers, such as evenings, weekends, busy periods or whenever your team cannot pick up.
